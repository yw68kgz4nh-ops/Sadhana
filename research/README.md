## Suggested record contents

- Research question and why it matters
- Evidence and source links
- Method and limitations
- Findings, counterevidence, and uncertainty
- Implications for the project
- Follow-up questions

Distinguish published evidence from project observations and hypotheses. Do not present an exploratory question as a proven result.
