## Working rules

- Carnegie Mellon University's Human-Computer Interaction Institute is the main academic reference for AI product design and human–AI interaction.
- This is our original beginner programme informed by public course descriptions and teaching guidance; it is not an official CMU syllabus, accredited course, or claim of enrolment.
- Keep all schoolwork in this folder, even when it connects to other Sadhana work. Link to related project records rather than scattering lesson files elsewhere.
- Each lesson should state its goal, explain ideas plainly, ask the learner to think first, provide examples, include a practical task, and end with feedback and a short recall check.
- Distinguish the learner's own work from hints, examples, and AI-assisted revisions.
- Adapt pace to evidence of understanding, not assumed ability. Mistakes are useful information, not a reason for embarrassment.
- No lesson is marked complete just because it was presented; record what was attempted and what the learner demonstrated.
