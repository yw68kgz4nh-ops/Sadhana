# Lesson 5 — Week 1 Review and Design Brief

**Estimated time:** 45–60 minutes  
**Status:** 🟠 Prepared study paper; not yet taught or assessed.

## Purpose

Bring together the first week's ideas: understand the user, frame the problem, consider AI capabilities and limitations, and compare AI with simpler alternatives.

## Review questions

1. Did I describe the problem before choosing a solution?
2. Did I label assumptions rather than present them as facts?
3. Did I compare a simpler alternative?
4. Did I consider what happens when AI is wrong, uncertain, or unavailable?
5. Can another person understand the brief without hearing the whole conversation?

## Peer and AI critique

Ask a reviewer to challenge the brief:
- What evidence is missing?
- Is the problem framed too broadly?
- Is AI necessary?
- Which failure case has been overlooked?
- What is the smallest useful test?

The AI reviewer should not simply agree. It should explain disagreements, offer alternatives, and distinguish evidence from speculation. The learner remains responsible for the final reasoning.

## Exit reflection

Write or say:
- One idea I can now explain.
- One idea that remains unclear.
- One assumption I changed or would like to test.
- My next practical step.

## Completion rule

Do not mark the week complete because the paper is read. Mark learning complete only after the brief is attempted, reviewed against the rubric, and revised where useful.
