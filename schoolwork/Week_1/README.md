## Expected Week 1 outcome

Create a one-page problem and AI-fit brief that identifies:
- the person and context;
- the goal they are trying to achieve;
- the current difficulty, supported by evidence or clearly labelled as a hypothesis;
- whether AI appears useful compared with a simpler alternative;
- at least two realistic AI failure cases and a sensible user response to each;
- the next question that should be investigated.

Do not design a large feature set yet. The purpose is to frame a real problem and make a defensible decision about whether AI belongs in the solution.

## How we will learn

Each lesson follows a supportive studio routine:
1. Start with your own observation or prediction.
2. Learn a small number of concepts in plain language.
3. Examine a worked example.
4. Try a practical task.
5. Review the reasoning and receive specific feedback.
6. Finish with a short recall check and revision card.

You may answer by speaking or writing. We will not mark a lesson complete merely because it has been read. We will record what you attempted, what you demonstrated, what remains unclear, and the next step.
