# Five Core Terms — Revision Card
Date: 2026-11-01
Document status: 🔵 Completed card; learning status: 🟠 In progress.
These are our five recurring terms selected from today's lesson, not an official CMU list.
🟨 marks key vocabulary; it is a reading cue, not a progress status.

- 🟨 **Human–AI interaction:** The study and design of how people understand, use, and work with AI. It includes goals, communication, control, uncertainty, accessibility, and recovery—not efficiency alone.
  - **Example:** AI suggests titles for Sadhana; the author decides which title to use.
  - **Recall:** What should the human do, what should AI do, and how should they communicate?
- 🟨 **User control:** A person's ability to direct, approve, stop, correct, or undo system actions where supported.
  - **Example:** Choose voice or typing, select the available reasoning level, and retain the final decision over a title or narrative.
  - **Recall:** Which actions can AI take independently, and which require agreement?
- 🟨 **Transparency:** Making relevant system actions, changes, capabilities, and limitations understandable. This does not require exposing private internal reasoning.
  - **Example:** Explain a proposed file rename and its consequences before applying it.
  - **Recall:** What changed, why, and what remains uncertain?
- 🟨 **Predictability:** Helping users anticipate what a system will do through consistent behaviour and clear expectations.
  - **Example:** A file retains its agreed name unless an authorised change is made; an estimated countdown is labelled as an estimate.
  - **Recall:** Would the outcome surprise the user?
- 🟨 **Visibility of system status:** Keeping people informed about the current state of a task with timely, accurate feedback.
  - **Example:** Show analysing, taking longer than expected, complete, or failed, with an actionable recovery step.
  - **Recall:** Can the user tell what is happening and what they can do next?

**Memory line:** Work together; keep control; explain changes; behave consistently; show the current state.

**Practice:** Describe an AI file-renaming interaction using all five terms. Answer aloud if preferred.
