# Experiments and Prototypes

Keep practical tests and prototypes traceable to the problem or research question they address. A test plan is not a test result; record results only after the experiment is run.

## Current experiment plans

- [EXP-001 — Active Thread Indicator and Thread Preview Usability Test](EXP-001_Thread_Preview_Usability_Test.md) — exploratory comparison of baseline thread navigation, an active-thread marker, and a non-navigating preview. **Not yet run.**

## Suggested record contents

- Hypothesis and research question
- Predefined success criteria
- Participants and task scenario
- Setup, dependencies, permissions, and steps
- Expected versus observed outcome
- Evidence and reproducibility notes
- Limitations, risks, and next decision

## Experiment integrity

- Do not invent participant numbers, results, or performance gains.
- Record failed and mixed results as well as positive results.
- Note small samples and other limitations.
- Link the experiment to the use case and any resulting decision.
