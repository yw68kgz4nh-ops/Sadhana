# Why We Chose the Name Sadhana

## The Sanskrit meaning

The name is written **साधना** in Devanāgarī and transliterated **sādhanā**. It is closely related to the Sanskrit word **साधन (sādhana)**, which dictionaries define with meanings such as a means or method of accomplishing something, carrying something through, fulfilment, attainment, or a process that leads toward a goal. In spiritual traditions, *sādhanā* commonly refers to a disciplined practice or path of practice.

Useful dictionary references:
- [Monier–Williams Sanskrit-English Dictionary entry for sādhana — Sanskrit Heritage / University of Hyderabad](https://sanskrit.uohyd.ac.in/SKT/DICO/index.en.html)
- [Monier–Williams and other Sanskrit dictionary definitions — Wisdom Library](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/sadhana)
- [Sanskrit Dictionary (Apte and other lexicons): sādhana](https://sanskritdictionary.com/?iencoding=&lang=&q=s%C4%81dhana)
- [Sanskrit Dictionary — साधन, with definitions including “leading straight to a goal” and “accomplishment”](https://kosha.sanskrit.today/word/sa/%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%A8)

The exact nuance depends on the form and context. It would be too simplistic to say the Sanskrit word literally means “landing and moving forward.” More precisely, the dictionary meanings around *means, practice, accomplishment, and progress toward a goal* provide a strong basis for the project's chosen interpretation.

## Why this is the right name for our project

We chose **Sadhana** because this work is not just about discussing ideas or using AI for isolated answers. It is about building a disciplined, continuing practice: learning together, doing useful work, reflecting on what happened, correcting mistakes, and making steady progress toward worthwhile goals.

For us, the name evokes two connected ideas:

- **A place to land:** a reliable place to return to, gather our thoughts, organise work, and regain our bearings.
- **A way to move forward:** a steady practice of learning, experimenting, improving, and turning intentions into completed work.

“Landing and moving forward” is therefore **our project's interpretation and guiding metaphor**, not a literal dictionary translation. It expresses what we want Sadhana to be: a dependable point of return and a practical path onward.

## A short description

**Sadhana is a place to land, learn, and move forward.** Inspired by the Sanskrit idea of disciplined practice and the means of accomplishing a goal, it is our ongoing effort to build a more reliable, thoughtful, and human-centred partnership between people and AI.

The name reminds us that progress is not one grand answer. It comes from returning to the work, learning from experience, and taking the next useful step.
